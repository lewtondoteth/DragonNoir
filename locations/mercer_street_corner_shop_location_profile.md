# Mercer Street Corner Shop Location Profile

Canonical bounded location profile for Mercer Street and the unnamed corner shop in the Toy Noir Dragon continuity.

Use this profile alongside:

- `../chapters/chapter_02_opening.md`
- `../locations/Toy_City_location_profile.md`
- `../rules/noir_narration_rules.md`

This profile preserves the stable place logic established by the current Chapter 2 opening. It should not turn Mercer Street into a fully mapped district unless future prose or an explicit issue expands it.

---

# 1. Core Function

Mercer Street is a rain-flooded Toy City street Dragon reaches after leaving the alley by his office building.

In Chapter 2, it functions as:

- the first named street movement after Dragon leaves the office
- a wet, shopfront-lined transition toward Harrow & Vale
- a place where ordinary low-rent commerce continues under bad weather
- a stage for Dragon's expense logic and street-level improvisation

The corner shop on Mercer Street is where Dragon buys paper straws, reads headlines, and uses the telephone to call a cab.

---

# 2. Mercer Street Atmosphere

Mercer Street should feel:

- wet
- lamp-lit even around noon when the weather is poor
- narrow enough that shopfront movement matters
- commercially ordinary rather than grand
- socially local
- inconvenient to cross in heavy rain

The street can flood badly enough that Dragon thinks of boats or horses on extremely high stilts as more plausible crossers than himself.

Use Mercer Street for street-level Toy City texture, not polished institutional wealth.

---

# 3. Corner Shop

The Mercer Street corner shop is a small, poky newsagent-style shop.

It sells or contains:

- newspapers
- paper straws
- boiled sweets
- bootlaces
- cheap polish
- cigarettes under the counter
- miscellaneous small goods people may not admit buying
- an ancient black telephone kept behind or beneath the counter
- an ashtray by the till, used for customers, arguments, and evidence

The shop should feel low-rent, cramped, practical, and faintly disappointed. It is not a chemist, apothecary, magical curiosity shop, or specialist repair shop.

---

# 4. Stable Spatial Logic

Stable assumptions:

- Dragon can reach the shop by leaving the alley and moving along Mercer Street under the shopfronts.
- The counter is close enough for Dragon and the shopkeeper to argue across it.
- Newspapers are accessible enough for Dragon to read while being watched.
- The telephone is controlled by the shopkeeper and must be produced from behind or beneath the counter.
- The shop is small enough that browsing without buying is socially visible.

Do not require blueprint precision. Preserve the social geometry: Dragon is a customer under suspicion, and the shopkeeper controls the shop resources.

---

# 5. Use Limits

Do not make the corner shop too whimsical or too important by default.

Avoid adding:

- magical stock
- jars, potions, or apothecary-coded props
- hidden back-room conspiracy
- specialist criminal services
- grandeur or symbolic weight

Unless future canon expands it, the Mercer Street corner shop is ordinary street commerce under rain pressure.
