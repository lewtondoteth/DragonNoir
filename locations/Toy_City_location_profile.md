---

# 1. Core Setting

Toy City is a full living-toy metropolis.

The toys are real, the city is real, and the story does not need to apologise for either fact.

It is not merely:

- a hidden bedroom corner
- a secret society beneath human notice
- a metaphor that needs outside explanation

It is a proper city with:

- streets
- offices
- districts
- rumours
- institutions
- economies
- repair politics
- myths
- underworld networks

Human-made objects shape the city, but humans should not drive the main plot unless a specific future outline requires it.

---

# 2. Atmosphere

Toy City should feel:

- rain-slick
- lamp-lit
- handmade
- theatrical
- toy-scaled
- emotionally worn
- practically funny
- crowded with small lives
- built from discarded or repurposed objects

The city can be assembled from:

- toy boxes
- stage flats
- old shop displays
- model-town pieces
- dollhouse architecture
- cardboard signage
- tin roofs
- wooden blocks
- velvet curtains
- railway sets
- repurposed human objects

The rain matters.

For Dragon, every case in the rain is personal.

---

# 3. City Logic

Toys in Toy City live, work, trade, repair, argue, remember, forget, hide, and fear damage.

Setting rules:

- damaged toys can be repaired but may return changed
- replacement parts carry emotional weight
- some toys remember where they came from and some do not
- being forgotten is a social fear
- being replaced is an existential fear
- being disassembled is the closest thing to horror, and should be handled with restraint

Repair logic sits near the center of city life.

Repair shops can function as:

- hospitals
- tailors
- mechanics
- confessionals
- gossip exchanges
- black-market clinics
- places of care
- places of exploitation

Good repair preserves identity.

Bad repair erases, replaces, repurposes, or distorts it.

---

# 4. District And Institution Handling

Toy City should feel large enough to contain:

- wealth differences
- repair economies
- municipal incompetence
- black-market exchange
- weather-exposed danger
- places where preserved toys are treated differently from worn ones

But no named districts, neighborhoods, institutions, brokerages, or civic bodies are fixed canon yet beyond Toy City itself.

If a future draft needs a specific district, market, archive, civic office, or repair authority, treat it as exploratory until separately approved and preserved.

---

# 5. Writing Guidance

Use Toy City as a living pressure system, not as painted scenery.

The city should affect:

- value
- damage
- dignity
- mobility
- weather exposure
- repair access
- professional risk
- who gets believed

When drafting city scenes:

- prefer practical details over abstract lore explanation
- let place reveal class, wear, fear, and social pressure through visible consequences
- keep toy-world logic sincere
- avoid flattening the city into generic noir backdrop

Toy City should feel complete enough that even small scenes imply larger civic life beyond the frame.
