# Outside Professional Thief Character Profile

Working character profile for the thief used in the opening music-box case.

This file preserves the thief's case function without pretending they are a fully built central character yet.

Use alongside:

- `../cases/opening_music_box_case_profile.md`

---

# 1. Current Status

This is a bounded provisional character surface.

Stable:

- the theft was carried out by a single outside professional thief
- the thief was brought into Toy City for the job
- the thief dies in a police confrontation before delivering the goods
- the stash is not recovered

Undefined:

- name
- species or toy type
- exact personal motive
- whether the thief privately understood the music box's significance

---

# 2. Case Function

The thief exists to make the case professionally shaped rather than petty or accidental.

They should imply:

- outside skill
- separation from ordinary local opportunists
- enough competence to pull off the theft
- enough interruption, betrayal, or hidden friction that the stash never reaches its intended destination

Their death should close off the obvious chain of explanation and force the investigation into the missing stash.

---

# 3. Writing Constraints

Preserve:

- single outside professional
- dead before proper delivery
- missing stash remains the key result

Avoid:

- overbuilding the thief too early
- turning the thief into the main hidden villain by default
- accidentally defining the unseen organization through the thief alone
