## Alternate Human-World Approaches

- Humans are absent and irrelevant.
- Humans exist outside the frame, like distant giants or weather.
- Humans are mythical to some toys and mundane to others.
- Humans created many of the toys but do not control Toy City.
- Human presence is treated theatrically, with flexible scale and realism.
