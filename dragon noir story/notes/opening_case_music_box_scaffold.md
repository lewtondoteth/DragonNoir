# Opening Case Scaffold - Stolen Music Box

Exploratory Dragon Noir opening-case scaffold.

This is planning material, not fixed canon. Use it to preserve the investigation engine and relationship dynamics for a possible opening case while leaving the larger mystery, organization, ending, and mythology undefined.

# 1. Core Setup

There is a music box.

It is treated outwardly as treasure:

- small
- portable
- old
- intricate
- emotionally resonant
- mechanically complex
- valuable enough to steal
- not obviously important to ordinary toys

The music box should feel like an object Dragon can understand immediately as treasure without yet understanding its larger importance.

It should also be plausible that ordinary thieves would see it as loot, officials would see it as property, the pink dragon would describe it as dragon treasure, and a hidden organization would need it for reasons no one is ready to explain.

---

# 2. The Burglary

A hidden organization or group requires the music box for some larger purpose.

The exact purpose is intentionally undefined.

The organization does not want to be directly connected to acquiring the music box, so it arranges or exploits a broader burglary involving multiple stolen valuables.

The burglary should look like a conventional noir theft case:

- several valuables are taken
- the burglary has enough ordinary motive to feel plausible
- the thieves may not understand the true importance of one item
- the official story can remain small while the hidden pressure grows

The burglars complete the theft and hand over the stolen goods.

However:

- the music box is missing from the recovered haul
- something went wrong during or after the burglary
- one or more burglars may have hidden, sold, lost, or privately kept the music box
- the theft accidentally destabilizes the larger hidden operation

The useful shape is a petty theft that has knocked something much larger off balance.

---

# 3. Pink Dragon's Approach

The pink dragon is connected to the hidden organization or operation somehow.

Do not define the exact connection yet.

Possible relationship types:

- participant
- reluctant associate
- protected asset
- blackmailed helper
- double-crosser
- former beneficiary
- informed outsider with her own agenda

She approaches Dragon privately and claims:

- the music box belonged to her
- it is effectively dragon treasure
- the burglary has given her a rare chance to recover it unofficially before the authorities reclaim it

Her claim should be emotionally plausible to Dragon even if it is incomplete, slanted, or strategically false.

Dragon accepts the emotional logic because:

- dragons instinctively understand attachment to treasure
- he validates the importance of recovering it
- he initially believes he is helping another dragon reclaim something precious
- the fee, curiosity, and her unnerving familiarity make refusal difficult

She should not explain the operational stakes at first. She lets Dragon believe he is working a treasure recovery case.

---

# 4. Layered Misunderstandings

Dragon sees:

- treasure
- emotional significance
- a noir theft case
- another dragon's claim on something precious

The pink dragon sees:

- operational necessity
- concealed danger
- a problem that must be fixed quietly
- a way to use Dragon's treasure logic and investigative skill

Ordinary thieves see:

- valuable loot
- a job gone wrong
- an object whose true importance they may not understand

The hidden organization sees:

- a missing component
- exposure risk
- an operation becoming unstable
- a private failure that must not become public

This case works because each group has a different genre in its head.

Dragon thinks he is in a theft case. The pink dragon knows it is also a containment problem. The thieves may think they are in trouble over stolen goods. The organization knows something larger has slipped out of control.

---

# 5. What Remains Undefined

Do not yet define:

- the final conspiracy structure
- the final assembled entity, object, ritual, or mechanism
- the exact purpose of the music box
- the exact mechanics of the hidden operation
- the ending
- the true scope of the organization
- the deeper mythology
- whether the pink dragon intends to betray, escape, repair, control, or expose the operation

The music box may later become:

- evidence
- a key
- a component
- a memory device
- a status object
- a repair mechanism
- a lure
- a bargaining chip
- something emotionally important before it is mechanically important

None of those functions are canon yet.

---

# 6. Investigation Engine

The opening investigation can follow practical noir questions:

- Who stole the valuables?
- Who arranged the burglary?
- Why was the music box included among ordinary loot?
- Where did the music box go after the handoff?
- Which burglar noticed its special value?
- Who is trying to recover it quietly?
- What does the pink dragon know that she is not telling Dragon?
- Why does official recovery make the situation worse for her?

Potential clue types:

- music-box teeth or gears
- winding key marks
- velvet lining
- polish residue
- tune fragments
- repair records
- pawn or treasure-broker ledgers
- box dust, thread, or drawer scent
- tiny scratches from being hidden in haste
- a witness who remembers the tune but not the object

Keep clues toy-scaled, physical, and emotionally textured.

---

# 7. Relationship Function

The case should strengthen the Dragon/pink dragon dynamic without prematurely solving it.

It lets Dragon believe he understands her:

- she is a dragon
- dragons need treasure
- treasure can be emotionally real
- outsiders underestimate that attachment

It lets the pink dragon use a truth to conceal a larger truth:

- the music box may matter to her
- the treasure claim may not be fake
- the recovery motive may be real
- the operational motive may still be more urgent

This is the right kind of lie for her: not simply false, but arranged.

The case should create moments where Dragon feels seen by another dragon and moments where he realizes he has been positioned exactly where she needs him.

---

# 8. Constraints

Preserve:

- noir investigation structure
- layered misunderstandings
- dragon/treasure emotional logic
- gradual reveal structure
- petty theft disrupting something larger
- the pink dragon's genuine-but-compromised care
- room to invent the actual larger mystery from scratch

Avoid:

- naming the hidden organization too early
- defining the final conspiracy
- explaining the music box's full function
- making the pink dragon simply innocent
- making the thieves fully aware of the larger operation
- locking in a final artifact, entity, mythology, or ending
- collapsing this scaffold into the active story bible as settled canon

The active value of this scaffold is the opening pressure: a small stolen object, several incompatible understandings of its value, and Dragon's treasure instinct leading him into a case that is not as small as it first appears.
