# Commissioning Party Profile

Working organisation profile for the party behind the opening theft job.

This file exists to preserve what is currently stable without forcing the larger hidden group into false precision.

Use alongside:

- `../cases/opening_music_box_case_profile.md`
- `../objects/music_box_object_profile.md`
- `../characters/pink_dragon_character_profile.md`

---

# 1. Current Status

This is a bounded provisional organisation surface.

Stable:

- there is a party behind the theft job
- the music box is what they actually wanted
- they used a broader burglary to hide that priority
- Odette is aligned with them in some way

Undefined:

- their official name
- their moral status
- their full structure
- their final story role

---

# 2. Known Behaviour

The commissioning party:

- wanted the music box stolen
- did not want to be directly connected to acquiring it
- used or arranged a broader theft so the target object would be obscured among many valuables
- now faces a failure because the thief died before delivering the goods and the stash is missing

They should read as:

- organized enough to commission specialized outside work
- cautious enough to hide the true target
- pressured by the missing object

---

# 3. Relationship To Odette

Odette is aligned with this party somehow.

That alignment may later resolve into:

- participation
- compromise
- protection
- coercion
- divided loyalty

Do not lock that down yet.

What matters now is that her cover story is shaped by this alignment and by the need to recover the object without revealing the commissioned theft.

---

# 4. Moral Status

Keep the commissioning party morally undefined for now.

Do not decide yet whether they are:

- wholly villainous
- partly justified
- defensive
- desperate
- corrupt
- protective for the wrong reasons

The current function is pressure, not moral explanation.

---

# 5. Writing Constraints

Preserve:

- hidden involvement
- indirect pressure
- plausible operational caution

Avoid:

- naming them too early
- over-explaining their purpose
- resolving their moral frame before the story has earned it
