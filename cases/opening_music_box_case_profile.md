# 1. Current Status

This is an active working case profile.

Stable enough to guide drafting:

- Odette hires Dragon
- the target object is a music box
- the public-facing case is stolen treasure recovery
- the deeper purpose remains undefined
- the commissioning party remains morally undefined

Do not treat the larger hidden purpose as settled canon yet.

---

# 2. Core Setup

Odette hires Dragon to recover a music box from a missing stolen haul.

The music box was taken as part of a broader theft from a vault, safe-deposit, or safe-style collection. Many items were stolen, which helped conceal the music box's true importance inside what looks like an ordinary high-value burglary.

Dragon initially reads the case as:

- another dragon's treasure recovery
- a valuable emotionally loaded stolen object
- a noir theft case with private stakes

The hidden truth is that the music box matters for reasons beyond visible treasure value.

---

# 3. Theft Shape

The theft was carried out by a single outside professional thief brought into Toy City for the job.

Key case facts:

- the job was professional rather than opportunistic
- the haul was broad enough to hide the music box among many stolen items
- the music box's real importance remained concealed inside the broader theft
- the thief dies in a police confrontation before delivering the goods
- the stolen stash is not recovered

This creates the case engine:

- the professional thief is dead
- the goods did not arrive where they were meant to go
- the stash is still missing
- the official case and the hidden case now overlap without matching

---

# 4. Public Story

The owner of the collection does not understand the deeper importance of the music box.

From the public point of view:

- a haul of valuables was stolen
- the thief died before the goods were properly recovered
- the loot is missing
- the owner places a public reward notice in the paper for information or recovery
- the music box can surface in an auction listing or catalogue as one item among many, described in dry institutional language
- the catalogue description should be scarce on details, noting only outward facts such as working condition, worn finish, age, or a faded maker's mark

This public reward should feel plausible and ordinary enough to let the hidden pressure remain hidden.

Auction or catalogue material should feel emotionally indifferent. It can make the music box visible to Dragon and Odette without making its true importance legible to the wider world.

---

# 5. Odette's Cover Story

Odette offers Dragon an unusually high fee under a plausible treasure-cover story.

Her stated logic:

- the music box is dragon treasure
- it is personally priceless to her
- recovering it matters urgently

The fee should work on several levels at once:

- professional payment
- emotional urgency
- status signal
- camouflage for the object's real importance

It should not read only as shiny treasure bait.

Her cover story should be materially true enough to survive pressure while still concealing that the original theft was commissioned.

---

# 6. Dragon's Understanding

Dragon takes the case believing he is pursuing a valuable or emotionally loaded treasure recovery.

He does not yet know:

- that Odette is aligned with the party that wanted the music box stolen
- that the theft was originally commissioned
- that the music box matters for a larger hidden reason

His likely path through the case:

- accept the case as treasure recovery
- investigate the missing haul
- notice or find some clue the police miss
- begin working around or against the police while pursuing the hidden stash

This should preserve Dragon's practical detective logic and his treasure instinct without turning him into a dupe alone.

In the Chapter 1 case reveal, Dragon can understand that Odette's attachment is disproportionate before he understands the reason. His recognition should come through treasure logic, professional suspicion, and observed pressure rather than through a clear explanation of the music box's hidden purpose.

---

# 7. Hidden Alignment

Odette is aligned with the party that wanted the music box stolen.

What remains stable:

- her cover story is designed to get Dragon looking for the object
- she needs him searching without revealing that the theft was commissioned
- she is not simply an innocent owner asking for help

What remains intentionally undefined:

- the exact nature of her alignment
- how voluntary her role is
- what she ultimately wants beyond recovery
- how much of the larger purpose she understands

---

# 8. What Remains Undefined

Do not define yet:

- the larger purpose of the music box
- the final moral status of the commissioning party
- the exact mechanics of the hidden job
- the final meaning of the stash
- the ending shape of the case

This profile is meant to support writing, not prematurely solve the mystery.
